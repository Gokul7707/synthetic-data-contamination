# Submit this paper to arXiv (free)

arXiv is the right venue. It costs nothing. A first-time `cs.LG` author usually needs a one-time **endorsement**.

## What to upload

Use the zip already built at:

`C:\Users\HP\Downloads\arxiv_synthetic_contamination.zip`

It contains `ms.tex`, `00README.json`, and `figures/*.pdf`.

## Steps

1. Open [https://arxiv.org/login](https://arxiv.org/login) and create an account.
   Use `gokulsrinivasan2020@gmail.com`. If SKCET gives you a college address, that is even better.
2. If arXiv asks for **endorsement** in `cs.LG`, send this to a professor or anyone with an arXiv paper in CS:
   > Please endorse me for cs.LG. I am submitting a sealed-holdout study of synthetic-data contamination in tabular and time-series pipelines. arXiv endorsement code: (paste the code arXiv emails you).
3. Start a new submission.
   - Type: **Computer Science**
   - Categories: **cs.LG** (primary), cross-list **stat.ML** and **cs.AI**
   - License: **CC BY 4.0**
   - Title: Detecting Synthetic-Data Contamination in Tabular and Time-Series Training Pipelines
   - Authors: Gokul Srinivasan Prabu
   - Abstract: paste from the paper
   - Comments: 7 pages, 6 figures. Code: https://github.com/Gokul7707/synthetic-data-contamination
4. Upload the zip (source, not only the PDF). Process. Preview. Submit.
5. After the `arXiv:YYMM.NNNNN` id appears (usually the next business day), add it to this README and to your resume.

Do not pay a journal that emails you an invoice. That is not a real CS publication.
